About Me

I am a Cal Poly Pomona Electrical Engineering graduate with extensive hands-on experience in bench-level prototyping, hardware verification, and custom embedded systems integration. My project work spans mixed-signal PCB design, automated test telemetry in LabVIEW, wireless PID motor control, and biomedical signal processing in C++.
