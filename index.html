ABOUT

I'm an electrical engineer who likes owning every stage of it: the sensor, the analog front end, the firmware, the filtering, and the wireless link that carries the data home. I graduated from Cal Poly Pomona with a B.S. in Electrical Engineering in December 2025 (3.66 GPA, Dean's and President's Honor Lists), after earning my associate degree in General Science with honors at Santa Monica College.

You can see it in every project below, each one prototyped and verified at the bench. For a semi-autonomous environmental-sensing UAV, I engineered a custom mixed-signal PCB, developed dual-camera terrain navigation, and built a ruggedized perfboard interface for flight. My wireless ECG monitor relies on a 60 Hz notch filter I designed by pole-zero placement in MATLAB; the interference traced back to a wall-powered laptop, and most of it vanished once the system ran on battery. Two wireless closed-loop systems, a PID-controlled 25,000 RPM motor and an LDR-driven LED dimmer, each have a LabVIEW console I built. And a PIC18F fan controller runs on bare-metal I2C, SPI, ADC, UART, and IR drivers I wrote myself.
